01 / ABOUT

A little about my journey

Education with purpose.
Research with impact.

I am an academician in Computer Engineering with experience in teaching, academic administration, research mentoring and institutional development. My professional work brings together classroom learning, outcome-oriented education, student mentoring and industry engagement.

As Dean of the Training & Placement Cell at Takshashila Institute of Engineering & Technology, Darapur, I work to strengthen students’ career readiness and build meaningful connections between academia and industry.

My academic journey includes a B.E. in Computer Engineering from the University of Pune and an M.E. in Computer Engineering from Sant Gadge Baba Amravati University. I am currently pursuing a Ph.D.
